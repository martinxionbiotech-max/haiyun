Equipment is 63–66% of what a flour mill costs to build; civil works, electrical, installation and working capital make up the rest. Buyers who budget from the equipment quotation are typically 25–30% short, and that is the most common and most expensive feasibility error in the industry.

Ranges are wide because building cost varies by a factor of three between countries.

## What pushes the price up

| Factor | Cost impact |
|---|---|
| Wet cleaning instead of dry | +8–12% |
| Purifier bank | +6–10% |
| Extra break passage | +7–8% |
| Stainless contact surfaces | +12–18% |
| Pneumatic conveying instead of bucket elevators | +10–15% |
| Storey tower instead of shed | +25–40% |
| High-altitude or tropical de-rating | +5–8% |
| CE certification | +6–12% |

## What pulls it down

| Factor | Saving |
|---|---|
| Fewer passages (with extraction cost) | −12–20% |
| Standard grey iron instead of stainless trim | −8–12% |
| Local building instead of prefabricated | −10–25% |
| Manual instead of automatic dampening | −3–5% |
| Single shift design (smaller intake) | −5–8% |

## The six forgotten lines

1. **Transformer and switchgear** — USD 30,000–150,000 at 100–200 t/d
2. **Effluent treatment** — USD 15,000–40,000 if wet cleaning
3. **Spare parts stocking** — 2–3% of equipment value
4. **Working capital** — 12–18% of project cost
5. **Import duty and freight** — 5–25% depending on country
6. **Commissioning travel and accommodation** — USD 8,000–25,000

## How to compare quotations

Never compare totals. Compare these five things on the same page:

| Item | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Flow sheet passages | Directly sets extraction and revenue |
| Sifter area (m²) | Caps classification capacity |
| Cleaning machine count | Determines achievable ash |
| Installed power | Determines transformer and running cost |
| Building dimensions | Determines civil cost |

Two quotations that differ by 30% and match on all five are a genuine price difference. Two that differ by 30% and differ on three of the five are different products.
