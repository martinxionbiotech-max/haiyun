## The rule

**Sifting area (m²) = daily capacity (t/d) ÷ 0.8**

| Capacity | Area needed | Practical configuration |
|---|---|---|
| 20 t/d | 25 m² | 2 × twin-bin (5 m²/bin = 10 m² each) |
| 25 t/d | 31 m² | 2 × twin-bin, or 1 × FSFG4×24 (26.3 m²) + twin-bin |
| 30 t/d | 38 m² | 2 × twin-bin, or 1 × FSFG6×24 (39.5 m²) |
| 40 t/d | 50 m² | 1 × FSFG8×24 (52.7 m²) |
| 60 t/d | 75 m² | 2 × FSFG6×24 (79 m²) |
| 100 t/d | 125 m² | FSFG8×24 + FSFG6×24 (92 m²) + twin-bin, or 3 × FSFG6×24 (118 m²) |
| 200 t/d | 250 m² | 5 × FSFG8×24 (263 m²) |
| 300 t/d | 375 m² | 7 × FSFG8×24 (369 m²) |

The rule assumes a standard 4B5M flow sheet at 76–78% extraction on medium-hard wheat.

Adjustments:

| Condition | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| 3B2M flow sheet (fewer streams) | −15% area |
| 5B7M+ flow sheet | +20% area |
| Soft wheat, low extraction | −10% |
| Durum or very hard wheat | +10% |
| Whole wheat, no grading | −40% |

## Distribution by stream

Total area is necessary but not sufficient. Sifting area must exist **per stream**.

A 60 t/d plant with 79 m² on two machines can handle:
- 1 sifter for break + scratch streams (4 passages)
- 1 sifter for reduction + flour streams (5 passages + tailing)

But it cannot handle eight independent products on one machine. High-square sifters solve this with 4, 6 or 8 physically isolated compartments.

| Machine | Compartments | Area | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Twin-bin FSFJ-D | 2 | 5 m²/bin | 20–40 t/d, 3B2M–3B3M |
| FSFG4×24 | 4 | 26.3 m² | 30–40 t/d |
| FSFG6×24 | 6 | 39.5 m² | 40–60 t/d |
| FSFG8×24 | 8 | 52.7 m² | 60 t/d and above |

## What happens when sifting is undersized

**Extraction falls.** Material that cannot be classified gets re-circulated or rejected as bran. Typically 2–5 points of extraction loss.

**Ash rises.** Improperly graded material carries bran into the flour streams.

**Power rises.** Recirculating material through the mill twice costs twice the grinding energy.

**Sifter overheats.** Overloaded sifter frames run hot and fatigue. Frame cracking follows.

Undersized sifting has no alarm and no visible symptom other than a plant that quietly underperforms its design figures.

## Maintenance that protects area

Sifting area is only real if the screens are intact and tensioned.

| Item | Interval |
|---|---|
| Screen frame inspection | Weekly, under strong light |
| Frame replacement | When mesh sags or tears |
| Hanger rod bushes | 12–18 months |
| Gyration check | Annually with a vibrometer |

A worn hanger bush changes the gyration path. Every compartment on the machine degrades simultaneously, and the effect is subtle enough to be mistaken for wheat variation.
