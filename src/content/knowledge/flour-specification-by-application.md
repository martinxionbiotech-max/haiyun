Protein is decided in the wheat, not in the mill: you cannot mill low-protein wheat into bread flour, and no amount of passages will fix it. What the mill actually controls is granulation and ash — and for noodle and biscuit flour those matter as much as protein does.

## Bread (pan / sandwich)

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Protein | 11.5–13.0% |
| Ash | 0.50–0.62% |
| Moisture | 13.5–14.0% |
| Water absorption | 58–62% |
| Development time | 4–8 min |
| Stability | 8–15 min |
| Granulation | 90%+ through 200 mesh |

Wheat: hard red winter or hard red spring, 11.5–13% protein. Milling: full 4B5M1S1T with [purifier](/equipment/purifier/); patent streams blended for consistent protein.

## Noodle

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Protein | 10.0–11.5% |
| Ash | 0.42–0.55% |
| Moisture | 13.5–14.0% |
| Water absorption | 60–64% |
| Granulation | 85–95% through 200 mesh, minimal coarse fraction |

Noodle flour needs **low ash and fine, uniform granulation**. Coarse particles produce speckled noodles and uneven hydration. Use purifier extensively and blend patent streams.

## Biscuit / cookie

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Protein | 8.0–9.5% |
| Ash | 0.45–0.55% |
| Moisture | 13.5–14.2% |
| Water absorption | 50–54% |
| Development time | 1–3 min |
| Stability | 1–3 min |
| Granulation | 80–90% through 200 mesh |

Wheat: soft wheat, low protein, low damaged starch. **Damaged starch is the hidden variable** — biscuit flour needs low starch damage, which means gentle reduction and lower roll pressure.

## Cake / pastry

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Protein | 8.5–10.0% |
| Ash | 0.40–0.50% |
| Moisture | 13.5–14.0% |
| Water absorption | 50–56% |
| Granulation | Very fine, 95%+ through 200 mesh |

Chlorination is used in some markets for cake flour to lower pH and improve crumb structure. Check local regulation before offering.

## Steamed bread (Mantou)

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Protein | 10.5–12.0% |
| Ash | 0.50–0.65% |
| Moisture | 13.5–14.0% |
| Granulation | 85–95% through 200 mesh |

Steamed bread tolerates medium protein and benefits from slight bran presence for flavour and colour. Medium-hard wheat.

## Atta (whole wheat, South Asian)

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Protein | 11.0–13.0% |
| Ash | 1.20–1.80% |
| Moisture | 13.0–14.0% |
| Extraction | 85–95% |
| Granulation | 70–85% through 200 mesh |

Atta is **not** a grade of flour — it is a different product. The bran and germ are retained, granulation is coarser, and the milling process is often chakki-style (stone or attrition) rather than roller.

For 6FGP-series mills, the granular Atta produced is specifically suited to chapati and roti.

## Durum / semolina

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Protein | 12.5–14.5% |
| Ash | 0.85–1.10% (semolina) |
| Granulation | 250–600 µm, narrow distribution |
| Moisture | 14.0–14.5% |

Durum requires separate milling with higher tempering moisture (16.5–17.0%) and purifier-intensive flow to produce clean semolina.

## The wheat-selection point

| Application | Protein needed | Wheat class |
|---|---|---|
| Biscuit, cake | 8.0–10.0% | Soft wheat |
| Noodle, steamed bread | 10.0–12.0% | Medium / medium-hard |
| Pan bread | 11.5–13.0% | Hard wheat |
| Hearth bread | 12.5–14.0% | Hard spring |
| Pasta | 12.5–14.5% | Durum |

**Milling cannot add protein.** It can concentrate it slightly through stream selection — patent streams run 0.2–0.5 points higher than the mill mix — but if the wheat is 9%, no flow sheet produces bread flour.

This is why wheat sourcing is a strategic decision, not a purchasing one.
